⚙️ How It Works

1️⃣

Upload Leaf Image

Drag & drop or click to upload a JPG/PNG/WebP photo of the affected crop leaf. Supports real-time camera capture too.

2️⃣

AI Preprocessing

Image is compressed, resized to 224×224 pixels, normalised, and fed into the CNN pipeline.

3️⃣

CNN Classification

MobileNetV2-based model classifies among 21 PlantVillage disease classes and returns confidence probabilities.

4️⃣

Treatment Plan

Disease-specific recommendations for fertilizer, pesticide, irrigation, and prevention are displayed instantly.
